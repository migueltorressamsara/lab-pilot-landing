What it does on day one

Automated from order to result. Auditable the whole way.

No rip-and-replace. The robot plugs into the process your lab already runs.

1

Pulls the order automatically

Your lab's LIS/EHR (Epic Beaker, Sunquest, LabWare, whatever you already run) sends the order straight in. No manual re-entry, no separate portal to log into.

2

Turns it into a robot plan

CogPhyAI translates the order into a step-by-step plan matched to your lab's own protocols. Not a generic script that assumes a different workflow.

3

Executes with vision and touch

The dexterous hand pipettes, moves samples, and runs your existing instruments. Tactile and visual feedback catch problems instead of letting them fail silently.

4

Logs everything, in compliance

Every action generates audit-grade video, timestamps, and reports. The result flows back into your LIS automatically, with nothing to reconcile by hand.
